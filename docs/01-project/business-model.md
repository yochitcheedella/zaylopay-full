# Business Model

## Confirmed Business Policies
- **Membership Fee**: One-time fee of **₹599 INR** (59,900 paise, inclusive of 18% GST as displayed on the payment receipt).
- **Immediate Activation**: Upon verified Razorpay payment capture, the applicant's membership is activated and a permanent **Affiliate ID** (`ZP-YYMMDD-XXX`) is issued automatically without requiring manual admin approval.
- **Payment Receipt**: Members receive a verified payment receipt displaying Order Reference, Payment ID, Amount Paid, and Affiliate ID, with both inline preview (**View payment receipt**) and PDF download (**Download Receipt**).

## Proposed / Future Business Vision (Not Yet Implemented)
- Integration with **Zaylo eCommerce** for shopping benefits, coupon sharing, and referral tracking.
- Commission eligibility, wallet balance management, and payout/withdrawal workflows.
- Multi-tier Admin and Super Admin operational dashboards.
